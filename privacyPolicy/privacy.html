Econo Camera Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

Econo Camera does not collect any personal data. Your recordings, MP3s and transcripts stay on your device or in a folder you choose. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores

Econo Camera saves the files you create (video recordings, converted MP4 and MP3 files, and text transcripts) only in places you control:

  • On iPhone and iPad: in the app’s own folder, shown in the Files app under “On My iPhone” or “On My iPad” › Econo Camera.
  • In any other folder you pick, such as iCloud Drive or an external drive. If you choose an iCloud Drive folder, Apple stores those files under your iCloud account and Apple’s privacy policy applies to them.

You can delete any of these files at any time from the app, the Files app or Finder. Deleting the app removes the files kept in its own folder.

Camera

The app records your video only after you start a recording, and it stops when you stop it. While the camera is on, your iPhone or iPad shows a green dot at the top of the screen (orange when only the microphone is in use).

Microphone

The microphone is used only if you turn it on for a recording, so that your voice is added to that recording. The audio is saved in the recording file on your device and is not sent anywhere.

Audio to Text

Speech recognition runs entirely on your device using Apple’s built-in speech technology. Your audio and the text made from it are not sent to us or to any other service.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No third-party code that collects data.
  • No sharing or selling of data. We never receive any data to share.

Sharing your files

If you choose to share a recording or transcript, for example by AirDrop, Mail or Messages, it is sent by the app you choose, and that service’s privacy policy applies.

Children

Econo Camera does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
