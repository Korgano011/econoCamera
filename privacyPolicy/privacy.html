Econo Camera Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Econo Camera does not collect any personal data. Your videos, photos, panoramas, cartoons, MP3s and transcripts stay on your device or in a folder you choose. Nothing is sent to us.

What the app stores

Econo Camera saves the files you create (videos, photos, panoramas, cartoon animations, converted MP4 and MP3 files, text transcripts, and Repository items with any notes you add) only in places you control:

  • On iPhone and iPad: in the app’s own folder, shown in the Files app under “On My iPhone” or “On My iPad” › Econo Camera.
  • In any other folder you pick for the Repository, such as iCloud Drive or an external drive. If you choose an iCloud Drive folder, Apple stores those files under your iCloud account and Apple’s privacy policy applies to them.

The app also keeps your settings (such as resolution and whether sound is recorded) on your device. You can delete any of your files at any time from the app, the Files app or Finder. Deleting the app removes its settings and the files kept in its own folder.

Camera

The camera is used only to take the videos, photos and panoramas you ask for. Video recording starts when you start it and stops when you stop it. While the camera is on, your iPhone or iPad shows a green dot at the top of the screen.

Microphone

When “Record sound” is on (it is on unless you turn it off in Settings), the microphone adds sound to your video recordings. The sound is saved in the recording file on your device and is not sent anywhere. Turn off “Record sound” to record video without sound.

Photos

If you choose a video or picture from your Photos library to convert or turn into a cartoon, only the item you pick is given to the app. If you tap Save to Photos, the app asks for permission to add items to your library. It can add items but cannot see or read the rest of your library.

Audio to Text

Speech recognition uses Apple’s built-in speech technology. Whenever your device and language support it, it runs entirely on your device. If they don’t, iOS may send the audio to Apple to turn it into text. In that case Apple’s privacy policy applies. Your audio and text are never sent to us. The first time you use Audio to Text, your device may download Apple’s speech language files.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No third-party code that collects data. The built-in MP3 encoder (LAME) runs only on your device.
  • No sharing or selling of data. We never receive any data to share.

Sharing your files

If you choose to share a file, for example by AirDrop, Mail or Messages, it is sent by the app you choose, and that service’s privacy policy applies.

Children

Econo Camera does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
