Econo Camera Support

Videos, photos and panoramas with tiny files, for iPhone and iPad.

Need help or found a problem? Email operafan01@gmail.com. Please include your device (for example iPhone 16 or iPad Air) and what you were doing when it happened.

For step-by-step help in the app, tap the ? on the Camera tab or in the Library.

Taking a video

  1. On the Camera tab, choose VIDEO above the big button.
  2. Pick a size at the top, from 60p (tiniest) to 720p (sharpest). The line under the sizes shows about how much space a minute will use.
  3. Tap the red button to start, and tap it again to stop.
  4. The video is saved in Library › Videos.

Photos and panoramas

  1. Photo: choose PHOTO, pick a size, and tap the white button. The photo is saved in Library › Photos.
  2. Panorama: choose PANO, pick a size, and tap the yellow button. Hold still for a moment, then turn slowly to one side and keep the phone level. Tap the button again to finish, or it stops by itself when the bar is full. The panorama is saved in Library › Panoramas.

Cartoon animations

  1. Open the Cartoon tab and pick a picture From Econo Camera… or From Photos….
  2. Choose a look, such as Toon, Comic Book, Pencil Sketch or Original Picture.
  3. Choose an animation, such as Wobbly Lines, Bounce or Draw It On, then tap Make Cartoon Animation.
  4. The animation is saved in Library › Cartoons.

Making files smaller

  • Pick a smaller size: 60p, 120p or 240p.
  • Lower the frames per second in Settings (the gear on the Camera tab). 15 is fine for most everyday videos; use 24 or 30 for fast action.
  • In Settings, set Video type to Smallest (HEVC). It makes files about 30% smaller than Plays anywhere (H.264).
  • Turn off Record sound in Settings for silent videos.
  • Use the Convert tab to shrink videos you already have, or keep just the sound as an MP3.

Audio to Text

Audio to Text, on the Text tab or from a video in the Library, turns what was said into notes. The first use may need your device’s speech language to be downloaded. If it doesn’t start, allow Speech Recognition for Econo Camera in Settings › Privacy & Security › Speech Recognition.

Camera or microphone not working

If the Camera tab is blank or videos have no sound, open the iPhone or iPad Settings app, tap Econo Camera, and turn on Camera and Microphone. Also check that Record sound is on in the app’s Settings.

Where are my videos and photos?

  • In the app: the Library tab, sorted into Videos, Photos, Panoramas, Cartoons, Converted and Text.
  • In the Files app: On My iPhone (or On My iPad) › Econo Camera.
  • If you turned on Also save to Photos in Settings, a copy is also in the Photos app.
  • Items you save to the Repository are kept in their own folder. To use a different folder, open Library › Repository and tap Change Folder….
