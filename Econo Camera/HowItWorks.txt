ECONO CAMERA — HOW IT WORKS
===========================

Econo Camera takes videos, photos and panoramas in small sizes so they
don't waste space on your iPhone or iPad. It also has an MP3 and MP4
converter, turns speech into text, and turns your pictures into cartoon
animations.

The app has five tabs along the bottom:

    Camera  ·  Cartoon  ·  Library  ·  Convert  ·  Text


------------------------------------------------------------
THE FIRST TIME YOU OPEN IT
------------------------------------------------------------

1. Open Econo Camera. It starts on the Camera tab.
2. When asked, tap Allow for the Camera.
3. When asked, tap Allow for the Microphone (needed for sound in videos).
   If you say no, videos are recorded without sound.
4. Later, the app may also ask about:
   - Speech Recognition — the first time you turn speech into text.
   - Photos — only if you save a copy to the Photos app.

If you said no by mistake, go to the iPhone's Settings › Econo Camera
and turn the permission back on.


------------------------------------------------------------
CHOOSING A SIZE (60p, 120p, 240p, 480p, 720p)
------------------------------------------------------------

The buttons across the top of the Camera tab set the size. The size you
pick (shown in yellow) is used for videos, photos and panoramas.

   Size   What it's good for                     Video, about
   ----   ------------------------------------   ------------
   60p    Postage stamp — shapes and colors      0.6 MB a minute
   120p   Very tiny — faces and big shapes       1.0 MB a minute
   240p   Small — good for most memories         2.0 MB a minute
   480p   Clear enough for most things           4.6 MB a minute
   720p   Sharp, still much smaller than normal  9.0 MB a minute

(A normal iPhone video uses about 60–400 MB a minute.)

Under the size buttons, a line tells you about how much space a video
will use per minute, or how big a photo will be.


------------------------------------------------------------
CAMERA TAB
------------------------------------------------------------

Above the big button, choose VIDEO, PHOTO or PANO.

TAKING A VIDEO
1. Tap VIDEO.
2. Pick a size at the top.
3. Tap the red button to start. A timer and the size so far appear
   at the top.
4. Tap the button again (now a red square) to stop.
5. The video is saved in Library › Videos.

TAKING A PHOTO
1. Tap PHOTO.
2. Pick a size at the top.
3. Tap the white button. The screen flashes white.
4. The photo is saved in Library › Photos.

TAKING A PANORAMA
1. Tap PANO.
2. Pick a size at the top. (The panorama's height is the size you pick,
   e.g. 240p = 240 pixels tall.)
3. Point the phone where you want the panorama to start.
4. Tap the yellow button and hold still for a moment.
5. Turn slowly to one side. Keep the phone level.
   - "Slow down!" means you're turning too fast.
   - "Keep the phone level" means you're tilting up or down.
   - The bar fills up as the panorama gets wider.
6. Tap the button (now a yellow square) to finish. It also stops by
   itself when the bar is full.
7. The panorama is saved in Library › Panoramas.
   If you stop too soon, you'll be asked to turn a little further.

OTHER CAMERA BUTTONS
- Flip button (bottom right): switches between the back and front
  cameras.
- Small picture (bottom left): your last shot. Tap it to go to the
  Library.
- Gear (top right): Settings (see below).

Note: the size, mode and camera can't be changed while you're
recording or making a panorama.


------------------------------------------------------------
SETTINGS (the gear on the Camera tab)
------------------------------------------------------------

- Size: the same five sizes, with a short description of each.
- Frames per second: 10, 15, 24 or 30. Fewer frames = smaller videos.
- Video type:
    Smallest (HEVC) — smallest files (best choice).
    Plays anywhere (H.264) — a little bigger, but plays on older
    computers and websites.
- Record sound: turn off for silent videos (even smaller).
- Also save to Photos: everything is always kept in Econo Camera;
  turn this on to also put a copy in the Photos app.
- MP3 quality: 32 to 192 kbps (higher = better sound, bigger file).
- Times in text like [1:05]: adds times to text made from speech.

Tap Done to close.


------------------------------------------------------------
CARTOON TAB — CARTOONS AND ANIMATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------

1. Pick a picture:
   - "From Econo Camera…" — your photos, panoramas or cartoon pictures.
   - "From Photos…" — any picture in the Photos app.
   (From the Library, you can also open a photo and tap
   "Turn into a Cartoon Animation.")

2. Look — scroll sideways and tap one. Each button shows what your
   picture will look like:
     Original Picture, Black & White, Toon, Comic Book, Pencil Sketch,
     Pop Art, Neon Glow, Old-Time Cartoon, Crayon

   Pick Original Picture to keep your picture just as it is, or
   Black & White to turn it into a black-and-white picture, and only
   add the animation (the button then says "Make Animation").

3. Animation — tap how it should move:
     Wobbly Lines  — hand-drawn lines that wiggle
     Bounce        — the picture hops and squashes
     Wiggle        — the picture sways side to side
     Zoom In       — slowly moves in closer
     Draw It On    — lines are drawn across, then color fills in
                     (with Original Picture or Black & White: a pencil
                     sketch is drawn,
                     then your real picture fades in)
     Rainbow       — the colors cycle through the rainbow
                     (with Black & White: rainbow tints wash over it)
     Pan Across    — slides across the picture
     Still Picture — no movement, just a cartoon picture
   Use "Length" to choose 2 to 12 seconds.

   Panoramas: Pan Across is chosen for you. Every other animation
   (except Still Picture) also pans across a panorama.

4. Size and make:
   - Pick 60p, 120p, 240p, 480p or 720p.
   - Tap "Make Cartoon Animation" (or "Make Cartoon Picture" for a
     still). A bar shows the progress.

5. It plays under "Your cartoon" (or "Your animation" for Original
   Picture and Black & White). From there you can:
   - Share it (the share button), or
   - Tap "Save a Copy to Photos."
   It is saved in Library › Cartoons (MP4 video, or JPEG for a still).


------------------------------------------------------------
LIBRARY TAB
------------------------------------------------------------

Everything Econo Camera makes is listed here, newest first, with its
size. The line at the top shows how many files there are and how much
space they use in total.

- Tap a group button to show only that group:
  All, Videos, Photos, Panoramas, Cartoons, Converted, Text.
- Swipe left on a file to delete it.
- Pull down to refresh the list.

Tap a file to open it:
- Videos and audio play at the top. Photos and panoramas show the
  picture (swipe sideways to see a whole panorama). Text files show
  the words.
- "Size on phone" and the picture size are shown.

What you can do with an open file:
- Photos and panoramas: "Turn into a Cartoon Animation."
- Videos:
    Smaller copy — pick a size, then tap "Make …p MP4."
- Videos and audio:
    "Make MP3 (sound only)"
    "Turn Speech into Text" — the words appear underneath, with a
    Copy Text button.
- Every file:
    "Share or Save to Files…" (AirDrop, Messages, Mail, Files…)
    "Save to Repository…" (see Repository below)
    "Save a Copy to Photos" (videos and pictures)
    "Rename"
    "Delete"

FINDING YOUR FILES OUTSIDE THE APP
Open the Files app › Browse › On My iPhone › Econo Camera. There are
folders for Videos, Photos, Panoramas, Cartoons, Converted and Text.


------------------------------------------------------------
REPOSITORY — KEEPING THINGS FOR LATER
------------------------------------------------------------

The repository is a long-term place to keep your best videos, photos,
panoramas, cartoons, MP3s and text, each with a note, so you can find
them again later.

SAVING TO THE REPOSITORY
1. In the Library, tap a file to open it.
2. Tap "Save to Repository…"
   (You can also tap the box button next to a new cartoon on the
   Cartoon tab, or next to a new file on the Convert tab.)
3. If you made an MP3 or text from it, leave "Also save its MP3/text"
   on to keep them together.
4. Type a note if you like (for example, who or where it was).
5. Tap Save.

The original stays in the Library. You can delete it from the Library
to free up space while the repository keeps its copy.

OPENING THE REPOSITORY
1. Go to the Library tab.
2. Tap "Repository" at the top. It shows how many things are saved and
   how much space they use.
3. Use the search box to find things by name, note, or the words in
   their text.
4. Tap an item to:
   - Play it or see the picture.
   - Read or change its note (tap Save Note).
   - Share any of its files.
   - "Copy Back to the Library" — puts copies back in the Library.
   - "Delete from Repository" (or the trash button at the top) —
     deletes it from the repository.

DELETING FROM THE REPOSITORY
- Swipe left on an item in the Repository list and tap Delete.
- Or press and hold an item and choose "Delete from Repository."
- Or tap Edit (top right), tap the red buttons, then Delete — handy
  for deleting several items.
- Or open an item and tap the trash button at the top.
You are always asked before anything is deleted. Only the repository
copy is deleted; anything still in your Library stays there.

WHERE THE REPOSITORY IS KEPT
- At first it is in the Files app › On My iPhone › Econo Camera ›
  Repository, with one folder for each saved item.
- To keep it somewhere else (iCloud Drive, a USB drive, or another
  folder), tap "Change Folder…" in the Repository and pick a folder.
- Tap "Use the Econo Camera Folder" to go back to the first place.
  (Items already saved in the other folder stay there.)


------------------------------------------------------------
CONVERT TAB — MP3 & MP4 CONVERTER
------------------------------------------------------------

Use this to shrink big videos (for example, ones from the regular
Camera app) or to pull the sound out as an MP3.

1. Choose a video or audio file:
   - Econo Camera — your own videos and audio.
   - Photos — any video in the Photos app.
   - Files — any audio or video in the Files app.
2. To make a smaller video:
   - Pick a size (60p to 720p) and the video type.
   - Tap "Convert to …p MP4."
3. To keep only the sound:
   - Pick the MP3 quality.
   - Tap "Convert to MP3."
4. When it's done, the new file is shown with its size. It also tells
   you how much space you saved, e.g. "Saved 180 MB (95% smaller)."
   Tap the share button to send it or save it to Files.

The original file isn't changed. If you shrank a video from the Photos
app, you can delete the big original from Photos yourself to free up
the space.

Converted files are listed at the bottom (swipe left to delete) and in
Library › Converted.


------------------------------------------------------------
TEXT TAB — AUDIO TO TEXT
------------------------------------------------------------

1. Choose a video or audio file (Econo Camera, Photos or Files).
   It starts listening right away. "Listening… (on this device)"
   shows the progress.
   Your audio never leaves the phone.
2. The words appear in the Text box. You can type in the box to fix
   mistakes.
3. Buttons:
   - Copy — copies all the text.
   - Save Changes — saves your fixes.
   - Share button — send the text file or save it to Files.
4. Turn on "Times like [1:05]" to add times to each part, then tap
   "Turn Speech into Text" again.
5. Earlier text is listed under "Saved text." Tap one to open it.

Text files are kept in Library › Text.

Note: the first time, the phone may download the speech files for
your language, which can take a minute.


------------------------------------------------------------
TIPS FOR SAVING THE MOST SPACE
------------------------------------------------------------

- 240p is a good everyday size. Use 60p or 120p for quick notes and
  reminders.
- Keep the video type on "Smallest (HEVC)."
- Lower the frame rate to 15 or 10 for talking or things that don't
  move much.
- Turn off "Record sound" when you don't need it.
- Leave "Also save to Photos" off so you don't keep two copies.
- Keep your favorites in the repository on iCloud Drive or a USB drive,
  then delete them from the Library.
- Shrink big videos from the Photos app with the Convert tab.


------------------------------------------------------------
IF SOMETHING ISN'T WORKING
------------------------------------------------------------

- "Camera access is off": tap Open Settings and turn on Camera.
- Videos have no sound: Settings › Econo Camera › Microphone must be
  on, and "Record sound" must be on in the app's settings.
- The panorama didn't work: turn more slowly, keep the phone level,
  and turn far enough before stopping.
- Speech to text fails: Settings › Econo Camera › Speech Recognition
  must be on.
- Can't save to Photos: Settings › Econo Camera › Photos must allow
  adding photos.
